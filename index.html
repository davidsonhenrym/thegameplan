I work in college athletics and have a front-row seat to an industry rewriting its own rules in real time. The transfer portal, NIL markets, revenue sharing, conference realignment — the structural changes hitting college sports right now are historic, and most coverage treats them like a news feed instead of a story worth analyzing.

The Game Plan is where I put the analysis I am doing anyway. Part public portfolio, part long-form research, part running record of what the data said while it was all unfolding. I cover football and basketball with a particular focus on mid-major programs, where every analytical edge actually matters.

About The Game Plan

The Game Plan is a sports analytics blog focused on the structural transformation happening across college athletics — the economics of the transfer portal, NIL markets, the House v. NCAA settlement, conference realignment, and everything downstream of those changes.

The Focus

The emphasis here is on the changing NCAA landscape: what the data actually shows about competitive balance, roster construction strategies, and how programs are adapting (or failing to) in real time. That means a lot of work on transfer portal valuation, revenue sharing allocation, and the gap between what programs say they're doing and what their moves reveal.

The Approach

Each piece is an attempt to use publicly available numbers — portal data, recruiting composites, advanced efficiency metrics, financial disclosures — to say something specific and falsifiable. The goal is analysis, not commentary. If a take can't be grounded in data, it doesn't belong here.

College sports is undergoing its most significant structural transformation in decades. The House settlement, revenue sharing, NIL markets, and an unrestricted transfer portal have rewritten the rules of roster construction, recruiting, and competitive balance all at once.

The Game Plan covers this landscape through data — not takes. Each piece is an attempt to use publicly available numbers and analytical frameworks to understand what's actually changing, and what's just noise.
